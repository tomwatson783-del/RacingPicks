Calculation Breakdown

Racing Model 2 framework

Horse-by-horse form within optimised windows; strength of opposition; class changes; winning margins adjusted for fallers; going, distance, course and handicap suitability; trainer–jockey records and layoff risks.

The model will estimate coherent win, top-two and top-three probabilities, and measure the first-ranked horse's gap over the second-ranked horse. No football xG formula is reused.
